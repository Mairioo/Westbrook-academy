OUR MISSION

WestBrook Learning Academy LLC is dedicated to bridging the educational gap for families in rural and underserved areas by providing an elite, flexible learning ecosystem. We support primary and secondary students who need a safe, laid-back, yet highly rewarding academic environment to thrive.

Our mission is to empower bilingual and Spanish-speaking families who are often isolated from premium resources, ensuring every child receives state-qualified instructional support and a personalized path to success.

100% State-Funded via Arizona ESA Vouchers

We believe financial barriers should never limit a child's potential. As an authorized provider, WestBrook Learning Academy integrates directly with the Arizona Department of Education Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) portal via ClassWallet. Qualified families can cover 100% of academy enrollment and distance-learning support with zero out-of-pocket tuition costs
